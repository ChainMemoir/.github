### Every wallet has a history. Understanding it takes evidence.

Transactions leave records, but records alone rarely tell the full story at a glance. ChainMemoir works to make Solana activity easier to follow, explain, and verify.

**Our principle: no important conclusion without a traceable reason.**

### What we're building

| Area | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Wallet Intelligence** | Explore wallet history and meaningful changes over time. |
| **Token Intelligence** | Put token activity and on-chain interactions into context. |
| **Recovery** | Examine supported recovery opportunities through a guided, evidence-focused process. |
| **Verifiable Evidence** | Connect observations with supporting on-chain records. |

Features and availability may change. Explore the current experience at [chainmemoir.com](https://chainmemoir.com).

### Our approach

- **Evidence over speculation.** Claims should be grounded in verifiable records.
- **Context over noise.** Make activity understandable without hiding complexity.
- **Clarity about uncertainty.** Unknown information stays unknown.
- **Security by design.** Never share your seed phrase or private keys.
